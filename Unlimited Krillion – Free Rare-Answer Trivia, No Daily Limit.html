        How to play Unlimited Krillion

        1. Read the prompt. A short countdown gives you free reading time before your air starts draining, and every prompt gives you at least 8 more seconds to think, even when your air is low.
        2. Type one answer. Spelling is forgiving: case, accents and small typos don't matter.
        3. Sink by rarity. Common answers add a few metres. Uncommon ones send you hundreds of metres down and refill your air.
        4. Watch your O₂. Oxygen drains while you think, and faster the deeper you go. A wrong answer costs 4 O₂; skipping a prompt costs 12.
        5. Dive again. When your air runs out the dive ends. Your best depth and the creatures you've met are saved in your field log.

        Tips for finding rare Krillion answers

        • Skip your first thought. If it came to you instantly, it came to everyone instantly.
        • Beware the Red Herring. Narwhal, Shiba Inu, Theremin: the famous “weird” answers are the ones everybody reaches for.
        • Go regional or specialist. A cheese from one valley or a fish from the deep sea (see our deep-sea creatures list) beats the supermarket shelf.
        • Don't stall. Thinking costs oxygen. A quick Shoal answer is often better than a slow Abyssal one.
        • Use the skip wisely. Skipping costs 12 O₂, about what a Shoal answer gives back. If you're blank on a prompt, skip early rather than burn air.
        • Learn from the answer sheets. After each dive, tap any prompt to see every accepted answer by tier, and how many other divers picked yours.

        Unlimited Krillion FAQ

        Is Unlimited Krillion free?

        Yes. It's completely free with no subscription, no sign-up and no daily limit. The official game's endless mode is a paid unlock; this one costs nothing. Play as many dives as you want.

        Is there a daily limit?

        No. Unlike a daily puzzle, you can start a new dive any time and keep playing for as long as you like.

        How is this different from the daily game?

        Daily rare-answer games give everyone the same short set of prompts once a day. This is an endless practice mode: random prompts, an oxygen meter instead of a fixed number of rounds, and no waiting until tomorrow.

        What counts as a rare answer?

        Every accepted answer is placed in a rarity tier, from Bubble (the obvious picks) to Black Pearl (one hand-picked gem per prompt). Rarer tiers sink you deeper and refill more air.

        How many prompts and answers are there?

        There are 109 open prompts with more than 13,000 accepted answers, sorted into six rarity tiers. Prompts are drawn at random each dive, so no two runs play the same.

        Is my score saved?

        Your best depth, number of dives and field log are saved in your browser on this device. No account needed.

        Is there a leaderboard?

        Yes. Your personal best is posted automatically to the daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards under a random diver name, which you can rename or take off the board after any dive. Only your best dive counts, and no account is needed.

        Does it work on mobile?

        Yes. It runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet or desktop, with nothing to download.

        Is this the official Krillion?

        No. This is an independent, unofficial fan-made game inspired by rare-answer trivia. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the official Krillion daily game.
